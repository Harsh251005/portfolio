I want to be genuinely good at this — not "knows the tools" good, but the kind of
good where you can hand me a vague problem and a hard constraint and I come back
with something that holds up.

The way there is building things that real people depend on, and being close
enough to the consequences to learn from them. That's a thing you do inside a
team that's shipping, not alone in a repository.

Longer term I want to build things of my own. I'm not in a hurry about it —
I'd rather spend the next few years learning from people who are better at this
than me.
