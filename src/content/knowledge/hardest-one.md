Voice, easily.

Text gives you time. A half-second pause while the system thinks is invisible —
the reply just appears. Nobody notices.

In a phone call, half a second of silence is a person saying "hello? are you
there?" and starting to talk again, over the top of the answer you were about to
give. Now you've got two people talking at once and a system that has to decide,
instantly, whether to stop or keep going.

Everything gets harder under that constraint. You can't wait for a complete
sentence before you start understanding it. You can't wait for a complete answer
before you start speaking it. And you have to be able to throw away what you were
saying, mid-word, without it sounding broken.

Text systems can be slow and still feel fine. Voice is unforgiving in a way I
didn't appreciate until I'd built one.
