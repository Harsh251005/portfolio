I explain what a thing does before I explain why it's built that way. People
mostly want the second one, and I've had to be told that more than once.

I go deep on a technical problem that's interesting and lose sight of whether
solving it actually matters to anyone. Curiosity is useful right up until it
becomes a detour.

And I'm five months out of university. There's a category of problem you only
learn by being on the other side of a system that broke in front of real
customers, and I haven't had enough of those yet. I know what I've read about
that. Reading isn't the same thing.

I'd rather you hear this from me than work it out yourself in month two.
