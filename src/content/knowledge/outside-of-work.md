⟨ NEEDS YOUR WORDS — this one I can't write for you, and it's one of the most
important on the site.

This is the answer that makes a stranger decide they'd like working with you. It
should have nothing to do with engineering. What do you spend a Sunday on? What
are you unreasonably into? What's something you're bad at and do anyway?

Specific beats impressive. "I make terrible filter coffee and refuse to stop" is
worth more here than a list of interests. ⟩
