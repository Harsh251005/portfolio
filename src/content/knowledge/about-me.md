## Where I'm from

Borivali East, in the north of Mumbai.

I'm happy to work remotely, to meet in person in Mumbai, or to move for the
right team.

## How I got here

On paper, I was headed for balance sheets. I studied commerce in junior college.
Off paper, I was building web pages, breaking them, and working out why they
broke. By 2023 the habit had won, and I went to study IT instead.

A few months ago I pointed that habit at AI, and for the first time I got serious.
Everything on this site came out of that.

## Education

- **B.Sc. Information Technology**, Thakur Shyamnarayan Degree College, University
  of Mumbai, 2023 to 2026
- **Junior college (Commerce)**, Thakur College of Science and Commerce, 2021 to 2023
- **School (ICSE)**, RBK School, Mira Road, until 2021

## Outside of work

I won gold at the National Karate Championship, representing Maharashtra. These
days I play cricket, and I love it. I watch the odd movie. And I try to get a little
better every day by learning one small new thing.

## Where I'm headed

I'm looking for an AI engineering role on a team that ships to real users. I'm
in Mumbai, and I'm open to remote work or moving for the right team.

Here is what I can already do, end to end:

- **Answer a phone.** A voice receptionist that books appointments in Hindi and
  English.
- **Show its sources.** A document reader that points to the page behind every
  answer.
- **Fix its own code, then stop.** A coder that retries from its own errors and
  knows when to quit.

What I want next is to be the person you can hand a vague problem and a hard
limit, like a budget, a deadline or a response time, and who comes back with
something that holds up. Longer term, I want to build products of my own.
